Rush Craft privacy and support

Effective November 21, 2026

Buds Enterprise LLC makes Rush Craft. Your island, resources, upgrades earned in play, and best distance are saved on your device. You can play without creating an account. Removing the app can erase local progress.

Optional purchases are processed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user ID, purchase records, and technical information needed to verify transactions and restore upgrades. Buds Enterprise LLC does not receive your payment card details. The game contains no advertising.

To ask about privacy, purchase data, or a support request, email Sanitation220@proton.me. Include your platform and order reference only if needed. Do not include card details. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request.
